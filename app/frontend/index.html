    Systems Topics

    A system is one team's support application. The bridge sends it messages on the topics it subscribes to, and accepts messages from it with its own key.

    A topic is a kind of work, like Infrastructure or Patient admin. Every conversation belongs to one, and goes to every system subscribed to it.
